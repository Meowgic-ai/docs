Platform integration includes two separate steps: connecting your store and integrating an app into its theme. Choose the platform guide that matches your current work.

## Connect your store, then integrate apps as needed

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Connect your store" icon="store">
    Install Meowgic from the SHOPLINE or Shoplazza App Store so Task can use real store information within the permissions you grant, and prepare for related work in Apps.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Integrate an app you created" icon="blocks">
    To let shoppers use a Section, Block, or similar feature you create on storefront pages, publish the app and complete theme integration. You do not need this step if you only use Task.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

We strongly recommend connecting your store first. You can also start directly with Tools or use Task to plan from the information you provide.
