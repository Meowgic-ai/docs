Meowgic is a place to get e-commerce work moving. Bring a goal to your AI team, turn a store idea into an app, or open a ready-made tool for the job in front of you.

Planning a launch? Imagining a new storefront feature? Need to finish something today? Start here.

## Choose where to start

### Task: Work with your AI team

Want to understand how sales changed this week or map out a new product launch? Start a Task.

Tell Meowgic what you're trying to accomplish and share the details you have. Your AI team coordinates the work, and you can review what it produces.

<div className="welcome-feature-visual">
  <ZoomableImage
    src="https://assets.staticmeow.com/meowgic/static/images/20260921/75fca334-a671-4028-8255-87ba897a0f43.png?x-oss-process=image/format,webp"
    alt="A merchant shares a goal while the Meowgic AI team coordinates the work in Task."
  />
</div>

If the task needs real store data, connect a Shopify, SHOPLINE, or Shoplazza store first.

### Apps: Build the feature you have in mind

Want shoppers to personalize a product, or give a new product a showcase that feels like your brand?

Describe your idea, and Meowgic AI helps you build an app around it.

<div className="welcome-feature-visual">
  <ZoomableImage
    src="https://assets.staticmeow.com/meowgic/static/images/20260706/d998a715-8938-4f10-8ae9-b4bcda8ffd7b.png?x-oss-process=image/format,webp"
    alt="A Meowgic FAQ Section in preview, with publishing and store installation options visible."
  />
</div>

Preview your app before publishing it. Apps that appear on storefront pages also need to be installed in your store theme using the steps for your platform. Publishing alone does not put them in front of shoppers.

### Tools: Ready when you are

Need a new image, a design extracted from a print-on-demand mockup, or product copy translated for another market?

Meowgic makes ready-to-use Tools for e-commerce work. Pick one, use it, and move on to your next task.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How are Apps different from Tools?">
    With Apps, you describe a feature you want and Meowgic AI helps you create it. Tools are made by Meowgic and are ready to use.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to connect a store before starting a Task?">
    No. You can plan and work from the information you provide. Connect a store when the task needs real store data.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does publishing an app add it to my storefront?">
    No. You can preview and publish an app first. To show a storefront feature to shoppers, follow the installation steps for your platform.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
