Meowgic integrations cover connecting your store, integrating an app, and connecting external tools. Choose the guide that matches the work you want to do.

## Choose the integration your work needs

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Connect your store" icon="store" href="#choose-your-store-platform">
    Install Meowgic from the SHOPLINE or Shoplazza App Store so Task can use real store information within the permissions you grant, and prepare for related work in Apps.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Integrate an app" icon="blocks" href="#choose-your-store-platform">
    Publish a Section, Block, or other storefront feature you created, then follow your platform’s guide to add it to the theme for shoppers to use. You do not need this step if you only use Task.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Connect external tools" icon="plug" href="/integrations/mcp-connectors">
    Use MCP connectors to let Task work with emails, documents, and business information within your permissions. No connected store is required. Available services and actions are listed in your account’s connector directory.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Choose your store platform

We recommend connecting your store before work that needs real store data. Complete app theme integration when you want shoppers to use a feature you created.

Once connected, users who only need store-related work can start with the [Meowgic Task guide](/tips-tricks/how-to-use-task).

## Connect external tools and business systems

To work with external tools and business systems in Task, see the [MCP connectors guide](/integrations/mcp-connectors). You can use connectors without connecting a store.
